What We Do

Two halves of one promise

Every response we run carries both pieces of our name: the hiil of standing beside a family, and the hoo of putting real resources in their hands.

01 · Hiil

Ending hunger, together

When drought and displacement empty a family's stores, our rapid-response teams deliver food baskets and advocate for longer-term food security.

0Meals Delivered /mo
0Partner Groups
02 · Hoo

Clean water for every home

We rehabilitate wells and boreholes and run hygiene education so that water-borne illness stops costing families their children.

0Wells Rehabilitated
0People With Access
03 · Hiil

Education for every child

Displacement should not end a child's schooling. We fund tuition, supplies, and temporary learning spaces in IDP settlements.
