Our Work

The story, mission, and people behind Hiil iyo Hoo.

Our Story

A name that means a promise

Hiil is standing beside someone — moral backing, defense, advocacy. Hoo is putting something real in their hands — food, money, resources. Somali communities have practiced hiil iyo hoo for generations through garab-taag, the informal relief networks neighbors build for each other in hard times.

We formalized that same spirit into an organization that could move faster and reach further: vetting local partners, tracking every dollar, and connecting the diaspora directly to the villages, camps, and neighborhoods where help is needed most.

Today we operate relief, water, health, education, and livelihood programs across Somalia, with a diaspora office in Minneapolis coordinating fundraising and community drives (ururin).

How We Work

Three commitments

01
Stand beside every family we serve

Hiil means we don't disappear after the delivery truck leaves — our field staff stay in contact with the households and communities we support.

02
Make every gift count twice

We pair moral solidarity with material aid, so a donation funds both dignity and survival — never one without the other.

03
Report back, every quarter

Donors receive a plain accounting of where funds went, down to the program and region, because trust is the only currency that matters here.

Our Reach

Protecting communities, region by region

From Mogadishu to Baidoa, Afgooye to the Juba Valley, our teams coordinate with local elders, women's groups, and health committees so help lands where it's actually needed — not where it's easiest to deliver.
