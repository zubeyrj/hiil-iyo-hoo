Protecting Communities

From Mogadishu to Baidoa, Afgooye to the Juba Valley, our teams coordinate with local elders, women's groups, and health committees so help lands where it's actually needed — not where it's easiest to deliver. Hiil iyo Hoo was built on garab-taag, the informal relief networks Somali neighbors have always built for each other in hard times — formalized so it can move faster and reach further.
